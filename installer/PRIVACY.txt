HT ISO Ripper - Privacy information

HT ISO Ripper does not collect, transmit or share personal data.

- No telemetry, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- The application does not connect to the internet. Disc imaging works completely offline.
- Settings are stored only on this computer:
    %APPDATA%\HTREASURE LTD\HT ISO Ripper\settings.xml
- Log files are stored only on this computer and are deleted automatically after the retention period you choose:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\HTREASURE LTD\HT ISO Ripper\Logs
- Disc images are saved only where you choose. Uninstalling HT ISO Ripper never deletes your disc images.

Setup itself connects to the internet only if this computer is missing the Microsoft .NET Framework, and only after
you agree. It then downloads Microsoft's official installer directly from download.microsoft.com over HTTPS, checks
its SHA-256 fingerprint and Microsoft digital signature, and runs it. No information about you or this computer is
sent to HTREASURE LTD.
